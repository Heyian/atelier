# Organizing features

Four features exist to keep work organized once you're doing more than a
single conversation at a time: Projects, Artifacts, Scheduled, and Dispatch.

## Projects

A **Project** is a persistent, self-contained workspace with its own files,
context, instructions, and memory — you group related tasks into it instead
of starting each one from a blank conversation.

> **Last verified 2026-08-10** — source: Anthropic help center, article 14116274
> ("Organize your tasks with projects in Claude Cowork"). Capabilities shift
> month to month: show the executive this date, and offer to re-verify
> against `references/sources.md` before they build anything on it.

One limit worth knowing: Cowork projects are desktop-only and stored
locally — there's no cloud sync for project data. If you build a project on
one computer, it stays on that computer.

## Artifacts

An **Artifact** is a produced document — a piece of writing, a small tool, a
diagram — that Claude keeps separate from the chat itself, so you can hold
onto it and share it. Artifacts are available on Claude on the web, Claude
Desktop, and Claude Code. Sidebar access and Claude-generated artifacts work
on the Free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans; Artifacts inside Claude
Code specifically is Team and Enterprise only.

## Scheduled

**Scheduled** work is a recurring task that runs on its own timer, without
you opening a conversation to kick it off. It's available in Cowork, on the
Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans. Cowork itself was noted, at last
check, as still rolling out — in beta on web and mobile, starting with the
Max plan and adding more plans over time.

> **Last verified 2026-08-10** — source: Anthropic help center, article 13854387
> ("Schedule recurring tasks in Claude Cowork"). Capabilities shift month to
> month: show the executive this date, and offer to re-verify against
> `references/sources.md` before they build anything on it.

## Dispatch

**Dispatch** hands a job off to run on its own: you message Claude from your
phone, and it runs the task on your desktop computer, using your local
files, connectors, plugins, and apps. The desktop computer has to be awake
with the Claude Desktop app open for the whole time Claude is working.

> ("Assign tasks from anywhere in Claude Cowork"). Capabilities shift month to

> `references/sources.md` before they build anything on it.

## Try it on your own

Open Cowork and look for Projects, Scheduled, and the option to dispatch a
task from your phone — availability depends on your plan, so what you see
may not match everything named above. Create one small Project and see what
it looks like to work inside it, instead of a plain conversation.
